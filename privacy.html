Privacy Policy — FCTOPIA Uploader

Last updated: October 17, 2026.

FCTOPIA Uploader is a private tool used by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit Google consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails, and visibility on the owner's channels. It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account data needed for those uploads.

How data is stored

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are used only to perform the owner's uploads. No tokens, video files, or personal data are sent to any third party.

What data is shared

Nothing. The app does not share, sell, or disclose any user data to any third party, and it does not serve ads or analytics.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page. Revoking stops all uploads immediately.
